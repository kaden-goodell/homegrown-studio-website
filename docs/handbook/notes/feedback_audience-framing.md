Homegrown Studio's craft experiences (Junk Journaling, Patch & Personalize, etc.) are open to **anyone** — adults, teens, all ages 8 and up, and any occasion — NOT just kids and NOT just birthday parties.

**Why:** Kaden's wife tends to add "Birthday," "Party," and kid framing to everything. Kaden wants that stripped. The audience is general.

**How to apply:**
- Don't put "Party" or "Birthday" in class/item **names** (e.g. "Junk Journaling", not "Junk Journal Birthday Party").
- Write descriptions in neutral, second-person ("you") terms about the experience. Don't frame around kids/birthdays.
- You also don't need to explicitly say "open for anyone" — just write it neutrally rather than counter-signaling.
- **If a source (existing name, your draft, a spec) frames one of these as a kids' party or birthday, ASK before running with it.** This is a recurring mistake to guard against.

Note: pricing/capacity model (whole-room private booking = capacity 1 vs per-seat open session = capacity N) is a SEPARATE decision — confirm it, don't infer it from party framing. Relates to [[offering-model-jun2026]].
