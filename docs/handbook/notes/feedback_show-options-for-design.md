When Kaden says a piece of design "looks weird" or "shitty" and one fix has already missed, stop redesigning blind. Offer two or three directions as quick sketches (AskUserQuestion with previews works) and build the one he picks. He decides fast and then gives precise follow-up ("put the links all the way across the bottom").

**Why:** On 2026-09-27 the footer took two failed attempts. The third, picked by him from three sketches, landed first time. He watches the local site live and reacts to what he sees, so he is a good judge and a poor spec-writer: he names what is wrong, not what he wants.

**How to apply:** one attempt from my own judgement is fine. After one miss on the same element, show options. Keep the sketches honest about layout; he chose the dark three-column footer from an ASCII sketch. See [[project_ux-review-sep2026]] and the user preferences on consistent styling.
