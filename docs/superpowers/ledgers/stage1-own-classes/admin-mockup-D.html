Admin: four directionsFour ways the admin could work. D combines the best of A, B and C. Tap through, then tell Claude which.

D. Home, with a tab bar

Recommended

A's home screen for the daily check, inside a bar of five tabs: Home · Calendar · Classes · Inbox · More. Only one sheet at a time: tapping inside a sheet swaps it for the next one. On the iPad the tabs become a sidebar and things open in a panel beside the page.

  • Why D: A's home for the daily check, B's calendar for planning ahead, C's tabs to keep everything organized.
  • Nothing is more than one tap from the bottom bar.
  • Never more than one sheet deep, so she can't get lost three layers in.
  • Every future stage has a home: parties, café hours and closed days now; staff and promotions already have a spot under More.
  • "To decide" catches what happened in Square without her: counter sales, refunds and classes short of their minimum.
  • Five tabs to learn instead of one screen.

Try: + Book a party · Calendar, then + on a day · Classes, Past, Kinusaiga, Refund · Inbox, To decide · More, Hours & Craft Café · switch to iPad.

A. One home screen

Open the admin and the week is already there, with big buttons for the jobs she does most. Everything else opens as a sheet over Home.

  • Answers "what's on this week?" without a single tap.
  • One big + New class button, with Block time and Print right beside it.
  • "Needs you" shows waiting requests, messages and teachers at a glance.
  • The calendar, library and full list are one step deeper, as sheets over Home.
  • Sheets can stack (Home, then Library, then a class), so on a phone she can end up three layers in.

Try: + New class, then "From a past class" · tap Potholder Looms, then Refund · Requests 2.

B. Calendar first

Reviewer's pick

The admin opens on the week. Tap a day's + to add a class or block time on that day, and tap anything on the calendar to open it. Inbox slides in from the side.

  • Tap a day and add a class: the date is already filled in.
  • One rule everywhere: tap anything and it opens. Classes, parties, Café and blocked time all work the same way.
  • She sees parties and Craft Café next to her classes while she plans, so clashes are obvious.
  • A full week is a long scroll on a phone.
  • Requests hide behind the Inbox badge, so she has to notice the dot.

Try: the + on Thu Oct 22, then "New class on this day" · Month · Inbox.

C. Like /staff

The same tabs-and-tiles look as the crew's door console: This week · Classes · Library · Inbox. Sheets slide in from the right, like the staff sheets on the iPad.

  • Looks and works like /staff, which she already uses at the door.
  • Clear tabs, with the library as photo tiles.
  • The least new design to build.
  • The tiles are made for checking people in, not for planning ahead.
  • Four tabs to hop between instead of one place to start, and the month view still needs its own design.

Try: Classes, then + New class · Library, then a tile · Inbox.
