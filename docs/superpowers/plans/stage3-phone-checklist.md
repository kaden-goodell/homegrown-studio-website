# Stage 3 — phone checklist (staffing)

For Kaden, on the live site **after** he says go. This stage moves no money and makes no Square write on the live site, so there is no real-money checklist. This is the check that phones, the shared iPad and Square behave.

Before you start:
- Use your own iPhone, plus one Android phone if any crew use Android.
- Have the studio iPad to hand.
- In /admin, pick a class tomorrow that you can put yourself on and take yourself off again.
- Tick "Tell them by text and email" only where the step says to.

## Your phone's calendar

1. **iPhone, on the phone itself.**
   - Open /staff › My shifts › Add my shifts to my phone › Get my calendar link › Show my link › Add to my iPhone calendar.
   - Tap Subscribe, then Add.
   - **Expect:** a "Hometown Studio · my shifts" calendar, with your assigned shifts in it.
2. **iPhone, from the studio iPad.**
   - On the iPad: My shifts › Calendar link settings › Make a new link › Show my link.
   - Point your iPhone's camera at the code.
   - Tap the link that pops up, then Subscribe, then Add.
   - **Expect:** it subscribes on the **phone**, not the iPad.
   - Check the iPad's Calendar app: there's no new calendar on it.
   - Tap Done. The link is gone from the iPad screen.
3. **A new assignment arrives.** In /admin, put yourself on a class tomorrow with Tell off.
   - **Expect:** within about an hour the iPhone calendar shows it.
   - To hurry it, pull down to refresh in Calendar › Calendars.
4. **A moved class.** Move that class 30 minutes in /admin.
   - **Expect:** the phone shows the new time, as one event, not two.
5. **Android / Google Calendar.** On the Android phone, follow the steps in the sheet:
   - In Chrome, open calendar.google.com, then ⋮ › Desktop site.
   - Next to Other calendars, tap + › From URL, paste the link, then Add calendar.
   - In the Google Calendar app, go to Settings › Hometown Studio · my shifts and turn on Sync.
   - **Expect:** it appears. Google can take up to a day to refresh; that's Google, not us.
6. **Catherine's whole-studio calendar.** /admin › More › Staff › Calendar link for my phone.
   - **Expect:** everything shows, and classes with nobody on are marked ⚠.
7. **New link and stopped link.**
   - **Make a new link:** the old subscription stops getting updates (nothing new shows). Delete it from the phone.
   - **Stop someone's link** in /admin: their calendar stops updating.

## Telling people

8. **Tell on.** Put a crew member on a class with "Tell them" on.
   - **Expect:** one text and one email. No colons, and the right day and time.
   - Test texts go only to Kaden, so do this with yourself as the crew member, or with someone who agreed to it.
9. **Every day of a camp.** Assign every day of a multi-day class at once.
   - **Expect:** **one** text listing the days, not one per day.

## The shared iPad (/staff)

10. **Header on a phone.**
    - **Expect:** the tabs sit on one row of their own that scrolls sideways.
    - The tab you're on is in view. Kits isn't there while kits are closed.
    - 🚑 Incident and your name are on the line below.
11. **Switch person.**
    - Jessica opens My shifts and the Calendar tab. Then Switch person › Sam.
    - **Expect:** Sam's shifts, Sam's ✓ You're on tags, and nothing of Jessica's.
    - Do the same with the calendar-link sheet open: switching closes it, and the link is gone.
12. **Unsaved times.**
    - Change "When I can work" and don't save.
    - **Expect:** an "Unsaved changes" bar with Save and Discard.
    - Tap another tab, a shift card, or Switch person.
    - **Expect:** "Your times aren't saved" with Save my times / Leave without saving / Stay here. Never a browser pop-up.
    - Pick a date under "Different on a date" without tapping "+ Add this date", then Save.
    - **Expect:** the date is saved too.
13. **Outside the hours they gave.**
    - A crew member marks "Saturdays: Can, only some hours, 4 PM to 9 PM".
    - In /admin, open a 1:30 Saturday party.
    - **Expect:** they show as "Outside the hours they gave (4–9 PM)", not "Not said".
