# Filing Calendar — Goodell Holdings LLC d/b/a Homegrown Studio

Who actually files what, and when. Goal: Kaden's personal recurring load = **one ~30-min MAT session per month** + two annual batch days. Everything else is delegated or eliminated.

## Owner legend
- 🤖 **Square Payroll** — auto-calculated, auto-filed, auto-deposited. Zero touch after setup.
- 🧑‍💼 **CPA** — annual stack. Ownership + fixed-fee quote is a walk-out deliverable of the briefing (`docs/CPA-MEETING-AGENDA.md`).
- 🙋 **Kaden** — the irreducible personal load.
- ❌ **Eliminate** — filings we can structure away entirely.

## Monthly — 🙋 ONE sitting, due the 20th (~30 min)

| Filing | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AL state sales tax | MAT | First return due **Oct 20, 2026** (September activity) |
| Madison County sales tax | MAT (ONE SPOT) | AL law (Act 2012-279) requires every locality to accept filing through MAT ONE SPOT — same session, same return flow |
| City of Madison sales tax | MAT (ONE SPOT) | Same session. (City also accepts direct filing — ignore, use ONE SPOT) |

Set a repeating calendar block: **15th of every month, "MAT sales tax," 30 min.** That is the entire monthly grind.

## Payroll — 🤖 Square Payroll files ALL of it (this is why we chose it)

| Filing | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Federal 941 | Quarterly |
| Federal 940 + W-2s/W-3 | Annual (Jan) |
| AL withholding deposits + A-3 annual reconciliation | Per assigned schedule |
| AL SUTA (UC-CR4) | Quarterly |

⚠️ During Square Payroll setup, confirm the **"file and pay taxes automatically"** toggle is on for both federal and Alabama. Needs the withholding R-number + SUTA UC number first.

⚠️ One-off: **Form 941 due 7/31/2026** (likely zero-wage — CPA to confirm/file this one, it predates Square Payroll).

## Tobacco — ❌ target: ZERO recurring filings

Two ways to kill the whole category; do whichever lands first:
1. **Source taxed leaf** from an Alabama-licensed OTP distributor → tax is prepaid in the price, no ALDOR tobacco account, no returns, no 12-hour invoice emails.
2. If sourcing out-of-state: when registering the ALDOR tobacco account, state the volume (~2 lbs/yr, tax ≈ $2–4/yr) and request **annual filing / de minimis treatment**. Ask Madison County (256-532-3498) the same question and keep their emailed answer.

Until resolved: forward supplier invoices to ALDOR within 12 hours of receipt (it's one email per shipment, a few per year).
