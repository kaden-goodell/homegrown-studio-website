### 1. What the Studio provides
Craft materials, tools, and instruction for the booked activity; table coverings to protect
work surfaces; setup and teardown of its own materials; and removal of craft trash generated

address visible spills of Studio materials.

### 2. What the Host provides
The venue, including adequate tables, seating, lighting, and access for load-in/out; a venue
that is safe and lawful for the Event; and any permissions required for the Studio to operate
there (building management, HOA, school administration, etc.).

### 3. Host's insurance
The Host represents that liability insurance applicable to the venue and the Event
(homeowner's, premises, or special-event coverage, as applicable) is in force for the Event
date. For Events of twenty (20) or more guests, or on the Studio's request, the Host will have
its insurer issue a certificate naming **Goodell Holdings LLC** as an additional insured for
the Event date and deliver it to the Studio before the Event.

Every participating adult must sign the Studio's Participation Agreement (which covers Studio
activities wherever conducted) before participating, on their own behalf and for their own
minor children. The Host will distribute the Studio's sign-up link to invitees in advance. The
Studio may decline participation to anyone without a signed agreement on file.

owners, employees, and agents from and against any claim, damage, liability, or cost

contents, or (c) the Event generally — except to the extent caused by the willful or wanton
conduct of the Studio. The Studio is not responsible for damage to the venue caused by Event
guests.
