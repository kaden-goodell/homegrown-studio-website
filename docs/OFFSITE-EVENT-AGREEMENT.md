# Offsite Event Agreement — DRAFT for counsel review (2026-08-03)

One-page host agreement for Studio-run events at a host's venue (school, office, church,
clubhouse, private home). Signed by the event host at booking; participants separately sign
the standard Participation Agreement, which covers activities wherever conducted. Everything
below the line is the signed text; counsel context is at the bottom.

---

## OFFSITE EVENT AGREEMENT

**Goodell Holdings LLC** d/b/a Homegrown Studio ("the Studio") and the undersigned
**Host** agree as follows regarding the event described below (the "Event"):

**READ THIS AGREEMENT CAREFULLY BEFORE SIGNING. IT INCLUDES AN AGREEMENT BY THE HOST TO
INDEMNIFY THE STUDIO AND CONFIRMS THAT THE HOST'S INSURANCE APPLIES TO THE EVENT.**

> Event date/time: ____________  Venue address: ____________________________
> Expected guests: ______  Activity: ____________________________

### 1. What the Studio provides
Craft materials, tools, and instruction for the booked activity; table coverings to protect
work surfaces; setup and teardown of its own materials; and removal of craft trash generated
by the activity. Studio staff will take reasonable care to protect the venue and will promptly
address visible spills of Studio materials.

### 2. What the Host provides
The venue, including adequate tables, seating, lighting, and access for load-in/out; a venue
that is safe and lawful for the Event; and any permissions required for the Studio to operate
there (building management, HOA, school administration, etc.).

### 3. Host's insurance
The Host represents that liability insurance applicable to the venue and the Event (homeowner's,
premises, or special-event coverage, as applicable) is in force for the Event date. For Events
of twenty (20) or more guests, or on the Studio's request, the Host will have its insurer issue
a certificate naming **Goodell Holdings LLC** as an additional insured for the Event date and
deliver it to the Studio before the Event.

### 4. Participant agreements
Every participating adult must sign the Studio's Participation Agreement (which covers
Studio activities wherever conducted) before participating, on their own behalf and for their
own minor children. The Host will distribute the Studio's sign-up link to invitees in advance.
The Studio may decline participation to anyone without a signed agreement on file.

### 5. Responsibility for the venue and guests
The Host is responsible for the condition of the venue and for the conduct of guests and
invitees. The Host agrees to INDEMNIFY, DEFEND, AND HOLD HARMLESS the Studio, its members,
owners, employees, and agents from and against any claim, damage, liability, or cost
(including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) the condition of the venue,
(b) the acts or omissions of guests or invitees, including damage they cause to the venue or
its contents, or (c) the Event generally — except to the extent caused by the willful or
wanton conduct of the Studio. The Studio is not responsible for damage to the venue caused by
Event guests.

### 6. Booking, payment, and cancellation
Per the Studio's standard booking and cancellation policy (homegrowncraftstudio.com/policies),
which the Host accepted at booking.

### 7. General
This Agreement is governed by Alabama law, venue in Madison County, Alabama; it is the entire
agreement about the Event and supplements (does not replace) each participant's Participation
Agreement; if any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stands. An electronic or typed
signature has the same force as a handwritten one.

## Notes for counsel

**Why this exists:** the Studio's GL (CSU quote 1047343542) carries CSGA4102, limiting
coverage to 525 Hughes Rd — offsite events run on the HOST's insurance (agent-confirmed).
Craft fairs where an organizer demands OUR certificate are out of scope (one-day event
policies, ~$100). Market precedent: mobile paint-party operators (e.g. Paint Your Poison's
published terms) use host-assumes-liability + hold-harmless; this draft adds the
participant-waiver requirement and the additional-insured mechanics they lack.

1. §5 indemnity scope: we carved out only willful/wanton (mirroring the participant waiver).
   Should host indemnity also carve out the Studio's *sole* negligence, or is the broad form
   enforceable as written under Alabama's clear-statement rule for indemnifying one's own
   negligence?
2. §3: is a representation of insurance enough, or should we require proof (COI) at ANY size?
   Business decision: we don't want to add friction to a 10-person home birthday party —
   the ≥20-guest threshold is deliberate.
3. Private HOMES: homeowner's policies vary on business-activity exclusions for events they
   host. Is the representation in §3 sufficient comfort, or should home events get a simpler
   acknowledgment instead?
4. Interaction with the alcohol plan: offsite events currently NEVER include Studio-provided
   alcohol (that waits for the separate licensee entity). Confirm §6/alcohol language in the
   main waiver doesn't imply otherwise offsite.
