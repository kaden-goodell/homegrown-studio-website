Hi, I'm Catherine, a former first grade teacher turned stay-at-home mom turned naptime crafter.

In November 2018 I bought an embroidery machine and started stitching names on things for friends and neighbors. Then I wanted a laser engraver. Then another machine, and another craft, and another. Kaden has said yes to every single one. He loves learning anything and everything new, so a new machine is basically a new toy for him too.

In 2021 we hosted our first party, and that's when it clicked for me. Making things for people was fun. Making things with people was so much better. We've had a few different names over the years, but it's always been about personal, handmade things with the people we love.

On {{openingDate}}, we're opening our own studio right here in Madison. We want it to feel like you're crafting in our home. Pull up a chair, grab some supplies, and make something with us. We love building community, and we hope this becomes a place where you find a little of yours.
